# PART I — PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to CORA (Community Report Assistance), an AI-assisted, web-based community reporting system developed to help residents of Barangay Purisima, Tago, Surigao del Sur, report and monitor community concerns.

CORA recognizes the importance of protecting users' personal information and respecting their right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how CORA collects, uses, processes, stores, shares, and protects personal information when users access the system, create accounts, submit community reports, or use its available services.

The system is designed to handle personal information in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and other applicable Philippine data protection requirements.

By using CORA, users acknowledge that they have been informed of the data processing activities described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect
CORA collects information necessary to provide community reporting services, manage accounts, and process submitted reports.

Depending on the features used, the following information may be collected:

**A. Personal and Account Information**

- Full name
- Email address
- Contact number, when voluntarily provided
- Account registration and authentication information
- Information provided through Google Sign-In, when used
- Account preferences and relevant system activity

**B. Community Report Information**

- Photographs of reported concerns
- Report titles and descriptions
- Detected or selected report categories
- Selected purok
- Geographic location associated with the report
- Date and time of submission
- Report identification number
- Report status and related updates
- Optional reporter information

**C. Device and Technical Information**

CORA may process limited technical information necessary for the system's functionality and security, including:

- Device or browser information
- Internet Protocol (IP) address
- Authentication and session information
- Technical and error logs
- Device-related information used for spam prevention

Only information reasonably necessary for the system's legitimate purposes should be collected and processed.

## 3. Purpose of Data Collection
CORA collects and processes personal information for the following purposes:

1. To register and authenticate user accounts.
2. To receive, document, and organize community concerns.
3. To assist in identifying reported concerns through AI-assisted image processing.
4. To verify the location of submitted reports within Barangay Purisima.
5. To enable authorized barangay personnel to review submitted concerns.
6. To monitor and update the status of community reports.
7. To communicate relevant report updates and announcements.
8. To detect potentially duplicated, misleading, or unauthorized submissions.
9. To maintain the security, reliability, and performance of the system.
10. To support authorized system evaluation and improvement.

CORA processes personal information only when an appropriate lawful basis exists under applicable data protection laws.

These lawful bases may include the user's consent, processing necessary to provide a requested service, compliance with legal obligations, or other grounds permitted by law.

Personal information will not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and any additional notice or consent required by law.

## 4. Camera and Image Collection
CORA requires access to the device camera when a user captures a photograph for a community report.

Photographs help document concerns and support the system's AI-assisted image analysis.

The reporting process is intended to use photographs captured directly through the camera rather than images selected from a personal gallery.

Users are responsible for ensuring that photographs are relevant to the reported concern.

Users should avoid unnecessarily capturing identifiable individuals, private documents, or other sensitive personal information.

CORA requests camera permission when necessary. Users may decline permission, but doing so may prevent them from using the photo-reporting feature.

## 5. Location Information
CORA may request access to the device's geographic location to determine whether a reported concern is within Barangay Purisima, Tago, Surigao del Sur.

Location information is used to:

- Verify that a report belongs to the supported reporting area.
- Identify the approximate or recorded location of a community concern.
- Assist authorized barangay personnel in reviewing submitted reports.
- Display the reported location on the system's map.

Location information will be processed for legitimate reporting and system administration purposes.

Users may decline location permission. However, reporting features that require location verification may become unavailable.

## 6. AI-Assisted Image Processing
CORA uses artificial intelligence technology to assist in identifying community concerns from submitted photographs.

The system's primary image classification model, MobileNetV3-Small, assists in categorizing reports into the following supported concerns:

1. Improper Waste Disposal
2. Streetlight Problem
3. Drainage/Flooding Concern
4. Road Damage

Additional automated services may assist with image verification, report description processing, and report consistency checks where enabled.

Photographs and relevant report information may be transmitted to the AI processing services necessary to perform these functions.

AI-assisted results are not guaranteed to be completely accurate. Images may be misclassified, and some reports may require additional verification.

Automated processing is intended to support the reporting process and does not replace appropriate review by authorized barangay personnel.

If submitted photographs are proposed for separate AI training or research purposes, CORA will provide any additional notice and obtain any consent or other lawful basis required for that processing.

## 7. Data Sharing and Authorized Access
Personal information and submitted reports may be accessed by authorized personnel responsible for operating and administering CORA.

CORA will not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

Personal information will not be publicly disclosed unless there is an appropriate lawful basis or the disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by law.

## 11. Account and Guest Mode Privacy
CORA provides account-based access and a limited Guest Mode reporting option.

Registered users may access account-related features, subject to authentication and system permissions.

Guest Mode allows individuals to submit community reports without creating a regular resident account.

Guest users may be required to verify their email addresses using a One-Time Password (OTP).

To reduce repeated or excessive submissions, the system may apply a restriction of one report per device per day.

Limited device-related information may be processed to support this restriction.

Guest Mode does not mean that reports are completely anonymous. Submitted photographs, email verification information, location information, and other report details may still be processed.

Guest information is subject to the same applicable privacy and security requirements as other CORA data.
